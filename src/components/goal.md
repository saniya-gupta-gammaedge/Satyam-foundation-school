Objective

Redesign the Satyam Foundation School website to give it a modern, premium, warm, and trustworthy appearance suitable for parents searching for a play school and primary school.

Important:

Do NOT change the website content or business logic.
Preserve all existing functionality.
Focus on improving UI, UX, responsiveness, animations, spacing, typography, and color consistency.
Keep the website lightweight and performant.
